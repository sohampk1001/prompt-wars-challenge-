General Elections 2026 Roadmap

Your interactive guide to navigating the world's largest democratic process. Explore the phases, prepare your checklist, and make your vote count.

📅 Expected Timeline (2026 Cycle)

Early 2026 Voter List Revision
March 2026 Election Notification
April-May 2026 Multi-Phase Polling
June 2026 Counting & Results

1. Voter Registration (NVSP)

Get your EPIC card ready.

To vote in India, you must be 18+ and registered in the electoral roll. Use the Voter Helpline App or NVSP portal.

  • Fill Form 6 for new registration.
  • Verify your name in the Electoral Roll.

2. Model Code of Conduct

Ensuring free and fair elections.

The ECI implements the MCC from the date of election announcement to maintain a level playing field.

  • Restrictions: No new projects or policy announcements by the govt.
  • Monitoring: Static Surveillance Teams (SST) and Flying Squads.

3. Nominations & Scrutiny

Candidates file their papers.

Candidates submit affidavits regarding their assets, criminal records, and educational qualifications.

4. Campaigning & Rallies

Reaching out to the masses.

Political parties release manifestos and hold rallies. Campaigning stops 48 hours before the end of polling.

5. Polling Day (EVM & VVPAT)

Casting your vote securely.

India uses Electronic Voting Machines (EVM) with Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail (VVPAT) for transparency.

6. Counting of Votes

The verdict of the people.

Votes are counted at designated centers under tight security and in the presence of candidates' agents.

7. Government Formation

A new term begins.

The party or coalition with a majority (272+ seats in Lok Sabha) is invited by the President to form the government.
